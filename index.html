Stay informed. Keep your focus.

Your agents are busy.
You don’t have to watch.

Following dozens of sessions takes time. Stepping away shouldn’t mean losing the thread. Prosody is being built to bring you the updates that matter, with enough context to understand what happened.

01 / Your rules

Decide what reaches you.

A result, a blocker, a decision, or a daily summary. Define when your agents should send you a message.

02 / One inbox

Stop checking every session.

Bring updates from your agents together. Catch up across your work without jumping between conversations.

03 / The context

Get the point. Keep the detail.

Listen to a concise briefing, follow along with images and video, and return to the original when you need to go deeper.
