What it does

A phone line to a session that's already running

Live session view

See every turn

Prosody tails your agent's real transcript and speaks its turns back to you — condensed into short, conversational sentences instead of raw markdown, paths, and tool chatter.

Approval flow

Approve from your phone

Consequential tool calls still stop and ask. Allow or deny by voice or a tap — your agent waits on your answer, it never guesses and it never auto-approves on timeout.

Self-hosted

Yours end to end

The gateway runs on a Mac or Linux box you own. Your phone talks to it directly over your own network — no account, no relay, no Prosody-run server sitting in between.

How it works

Four steps, all of them on your own machines

01

Run the gateway

A small background service runs on each machine hosting a coding session — a Mac mini, a laptop, a workstation. It reads your agent's session state directly; nothing is uploaded anywhere to make this work.

02

Pair your phone

Enter a pairing code once. The app and your gateway trust each other over an encrypted connection from then on — no account, no login, no identity anyone but you manages.

03

Walk away

Keep talking to your session over your own Wi-Fi. Hear it work, speak your replies, glance at the screen only when you want to. A private tailnet for reaching it away from home is next.

04

Approve what matters

Every consequential step still stops and asks before it runs. Prosody adds a voice channel to that decision — it doesn't remove the decision.
